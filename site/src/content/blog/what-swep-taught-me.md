As part of my engineering training I took part in the **Student Work Experience Program (SWEP)**, which rotated me through different departments.

## Where I worked

- **Agricultural Engineering:** risk assessments of workshops, and housekeeping for safety compliance.
- **Chemical Engineering:** safety inspections and lab housekeeping.
- **Civil Engineering:** risk assessments of labs and keeping work areas safe.

## The big takeaway

Engineering isn't only design and equations. It happens in real spaces with real hazards, and a safe workshop is everyone's job. Working across departments also showed me how much good engineering depends on people from different disciplines cooperating.
