Hi, I'm Aliyah. I study Mechatronics Engineering at LASUSTECH, and I'm deeply interested in AI, robotics and embedded systems.

I've always tried to learn in the open. I write down coursework takeaways, share my learning roadmaps with friends, and keep track of what worked and what didn't. This log is where all of that will live from now on.

## What you'll find here

- **Builds.** Arduino projects, robotics experiments and anything with a sensor attached.
- **Workshops and events.** What I'm organising with RAIN-INN and other communities on campus.
- **Coursework notes.** The ideas from class that actually stuck.
- **Lessons learned.** Including the ones I learned the hard way.

One small thing: this site picks a new look every time you load it. I couldn't choose one style, so I kept six and let them mix. Refresh and see what you get.
