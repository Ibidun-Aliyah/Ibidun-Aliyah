In 2025 I became campus lead for the **Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Innovators Network (RAIN-INN)** at LASUSTECH. The goal is simple to say and harder to do: build a student-driven robotics and AI community on campus.

## What that looks like

- **Hands-on workshops.** We run AI and machine learning workshops for complete beginners, open to students from different departments, not just engineering.
- **Peer learning.** People learn faster together, so we lean on study groups and hackathons.
- **Working with mentors.** I collaborate with RAIN-INN's national leadership and local mentors so our campus projects line up with what industry actually needs.

## What's next

I've proposed LASUSTECH's first hackathon, focused on problems specific to our campus. Planning is underway, and I'll write about how it goes here.
